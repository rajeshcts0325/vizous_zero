WARDROBE STYLES

Configurations Built Around Your Room Dimensions

Each wardrobe style is selected based on available floor clearance, bedroom layout, access preferences, and overall project scope.

Style 01

Sliding-Door Wardrobes

Glides smoothly along precision tracks without requiring swing clearance in front of the bed—ideal for compact bedrooms and contemporary master suites.

Style 02

Hinged-Door Wardrobes

Provides full, simultaneous visibility of the entire wardrobe interior and supports L-shaped corner configurations and classic or modern profile handles.

Style 03

Floor-to-Ceiling Wardrobes

Extends seamlessly up to the ceiling with integrated upper loft compartments, eliminating dust-collecting gaps and maximizing vertical storage for luggage and seasonal bedding.

Style 04

Walk-In Wardrobe Concepts

Dedicated dressing zones or partitioned bedroom suites featuring open shelving, tinted glass shutters, island accessory drawers, and ambient lighting.

Style 05

Wardrobes with Integrated Dressing Units

Combines full-length mirrors, cosmetics shelving, concealed vanity drawers, and lighting into a single cohesive wall elevation—saving floor space while keeping daily grooming essentials organized.

INSIDE YOUR WARDROBE

Smart Internal Organization

A wardrobe’s true value lies inside. We map out internal compartments around your actual wardrobe inventory—whether you store formal suits, sarees, daily wear, or travel gear:

Hanging Sections

Long-hang zones for coats, gowns, and sarees plus double-hang rods for shirts and trousers.

Adjustable Shelves

ergonomically spaced cubbies for folded garments and everyday essentials.

Lockable Drawer Units

Smooth soft-close drawers with secure locks for valuables and documents.

Accessory Organizers

Sectioned pull-out trays for watches, ties, belts, and jewelry.

Shoe & Bag Storage

Dedicated lower pull-out racks and tall compartments for handbags and footwear.

Linen & Seasonal Lofts

High-capacity upper compartments for comforters, winter wear, and suitcases.

Design & Finish Choices

Coordinate your wardrobe doors with your bed headboard and wall palette. Choose from warm wood-grain laminates, matte or gloss finishes, fluted paneling, tinted or bronzed mirror inserts, slim aluminum door profiles, and concealed soft-close hardware.
