Floating TV Console

A slim wall-hung console that keeps the floor clear and makes the room feel larger. Easy to clean and a good fit when you want clean lines.

  1. 1
    Hidden cabling

    Power and HDMI run behind the panel, so no wires show.

  2. 2
    Soft-close drawers

    Space for remotes, consoles and set-top boxes.

  3. 3
    Underglow LED

    A soft glow that makes the unit appear to float.

Full-height Media Wall

A floor-to-ceiling composition that holds the TV, storage and display together. The statement piece for a main living room.

    Hide blankets, games and everyday clutter behind doors.

Fluted Panel Wall

  2. 2

    The screen sits close to the wall with cables hidden.
