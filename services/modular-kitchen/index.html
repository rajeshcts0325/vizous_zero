TAILORED MODULES

Comprehensive Customization Options

Every cabinet module is built to fit your exact storage inventory, appliance sizes, and reach preferences:

  • 1 Base Cabinets & Wall Cabinets: Ergonomic depth and height configurations with lift-up, hinged, or fluted glass shutters.
  • 2 Tall Storage & Pantry Units: Pull-out larder systems and multi-shelf tall units that keep dry groceries organized and visible.
  • 3 Drawer Organizers & Internal Storage: Dedicated cutlery trays, thali/plate baskets, spice pull-outs, and under-sink organizers.
  • 4 Corner Storage Solutions: Magic corners, carousel trays, and S-carousel units that make deep blind corners effortless to access.
  • 5 Appliance Integration: Built-in housing for chimneys, hobs, ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, and refrigerators.
  • 6 Countertop, Backsplash & Profile Details: Coordinated worktops, easy-clean backsplashes, G-profile handleless channels, or classic pulls.

MATERIAL & FINISH SELECTION

Balancing Aesthetics, Durability & Maintenance

Selecting the right combination of core boards, shutter finishes, hardware, and countertops directly influences how your kitchen ages, how easily it wipes clean, and your overall project budget:

Cabinet Core Materials

Moisture-resistant and boiling-water-resistant engineered ply and board options selected based on wet-zone (sink) versus dry-zone placement.

Surface Finishes

Choose from high-gloss or matte laminates, sleek acrylics, membrane finishes, or PU-coated surfaces depending on your desired sheen, scratch resistance, and budget.

Hardware & Worktop Coordination

Precision hinges and soft-close drawer channels (including leading brands like Hettich) paired with durable granite or quartz worktops and task lighting.
